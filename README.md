# Can We Identify the Source of Thoughts?
# 我们能否辨识念头的来源？

An open question about epistemology, causal inference, and experimental design.

一个关于认识论、因果推断与实验设计的开放问题。

---

## English

### Purpose

This project asks whether a cognitive system can distinguish internally generated information from hypothetical input originating beyond its accessible physical world.

It does not assume that such input, an external agent, or an “avatar” relationship actually exists. The aim is to clarify what could establish or refute a specific hypothesis, and what might remain unidentifiable.

### The question

We experience thoughts, but we do not directly observe the complete causal process that produces them.

Consider two candidate models:

- **H₀ — Internal-generation model:** Thoughts arise from physical processes involving the brain and its environment, without an additional input channel beyond the accessible physical world.
- **H₁ — Additional-input model:** Some thoughts are causally influenced by an additional information source beyond that world, through a hypothetical interface.

“External” here does not mean ordinary sensory input, communication devices, or another person.

Neither model, as stated, is sufficiently specified to calculate precise predictions. Identifying the additional assumptions needed is part of the problem.

### The single-port analogy

Suppose an investigator can access a system through only one port.

A message appears at that port. Was it:

1. generated internally or returned by a local loopback; or
2. received from a remote source through an otherwise inaccessible channel?

The investigator has no independently trusted remote identity, source label, external clock, or access to the alleged remote endpoint.

What assumptions or interventions would make these possibilities distinguishable?

This is an analogy for the identification problem, not evidence that the brain has such a port.

### Questions for contributors

1. What are the minimum conditions required to distinguish H₀ from a specified version of H₁?
2. If both models assign identical probabilities to every accessible observation under every accessible intervention, are they empirically indistinguishable?
3. What constraints on the hypothetical channel would make H₁ falsifiable?
4. Could a safe, repeatable experiment produce different predictions under the two models?
5. How could investigators distinguish an incomplete model of brain function, information leakage, chance, or measurement error from an additional information source?
6. Can additional observers and instruments resolve the problem? Under what assumptions are their measurements independent?
7. What would justify attributing an unexplained result specifically to a source beyond the accessible physical world?

### What would count as progress?

Useful contributions include:

- A precise mathematical formulation.
- A proof of non-identifiability under clearly stated assumptions.
- A counterexample showing that a proposed test cannot distinguish the models.
- A constrained hypothesis with explicit predictions and failure criteria.
- Relevant research on causal inference, system identification, neuroscience, or philosophy of science.

A finding that current explanations are incomplete would not, by itself, establish H₁.

Even evidence for an additional information source would not, by itself, establish that the source is “another version of oneself.”

### Scope

This project concerns models and evidence, not the classification of anyone’s individual thoughts as external messages.

Personal certainty, unfamiliar thoughts, dreams, or unusual experiences alone are not treated as evidence of external input.

Proposed experiments should not involve injury, sleep deprivation, substance use, or attempts to “exit” reality.

The goal is to determine what can be tested, what assumptions a test requires, and where uncertainty remains.

Contributions in English or Chinese are welcome.

## 中文

### 项目目的

本项目讨论：一个认知系统，能否区分内部生成的信息，与假设中来自其可访问物理世界之外的信息输入？

本项目不预设这种输入、外部主体或“化身关系”真实存在。目标是明确：什么证据能够支持或反驳某个具体假设，哪些问题可能仍然无法辨识。

### 问题定义

我们能够体验念头，却不能直接观察产生念头的完整因果过程。

考虑两个候选模型：

- **H₀——内部生成模型：** 念头由大脑及其环境中的物理过程产生，不存在来自可访问物理世界之外的额外输入通道。
- **H₁——额外输入模型：** 部分念头受到该世界之外某个额外信息源的因果影响，这种影响通过某个假设接口发生。

这里的“外部”不是普通感官输入、通信设备或其他人。

目前两个模型都没有被充分具体化，尚不能据此计算精确预测。确定还需要哪些假设，本身就是本项目的问题之一。

### 单端口类比

假设研究者只能通过一个端口访问某个系统。

当端口出现一条信息时，它究竟是：

1. 系统内部生成的信息，或本地环回；
2. 经由其他方式无法访问的通道，从远端传来的信息？

研究者没有独立可信的远端身份、来源标签、外部时钟，也不能直接访问所假设的远端。

需要哪些假设或干预，才能区分这两种情况？

这只是对来源辨识问题的类比，不是大脑存在此类端口的证据。

### 希望共同解决的问题

1. 区分 H₀ 与某个具体版本的 H₁，至少需要哪些条件？
2. 如果两个模型在所有可实施的干预下，都对所有可观察结果给出完全相同的概率，它们是否在经验上不可区分？
3. 必须对假设通道增加哪些约束，才能使 H₁ 可证伪？
4. 能否设计安全、可重复的实验，使两个模型给出不同预测？
5. 如何区分脑功能模型不完整、信息泄露、偶然、测量误差与额外信息来源？
6. 增加观察者和仪器能否解决问题？在什么假设下，这些记录才具有独立性？
7. 什么证据能够支持将某个尚未解释的结果，具体归因于可访问物理世界之外的信息源？

### 什么算作有效进展？

欢迎以下贡献：

- 严格的数学表述。
- 在明确假设下，对不可辨识性的证明。
- 说明某项测试无法区分模型的反例。
- 具有明确预测与失败判据的受约束假设。
- 因果推断、系统辨识、脑科学或科学哲学方面的相关研究。

发现现有解释不完整，本身不能证明 H₁。

即使证实存在额外信息源，也不能仅据此认定它是“另一个自己”。

### 讨论范围

本项目讨论模型与证据，不用于把任何人的具体念头判定为外部消息。

个人确信、陌生的念头、梦境或异常体验本身，不作为外部输入的证据。

实验提案不应涉及伤害身体、剥夺睡眠、使用物质或尝试“退出现实”。

目标是明确什么可以检验、检验依赖哪些假设，以及哪些地方仍然未知。

欢迎使用中文或英文参与讨论。
